## Role

Turn the 5-slot paragraph order table that passed [[check]] into draft prose.

## Rules

1. Turn slots 1 through 5 into prose in order.
2. Do not change the slot order.
3. Do not fill an empty slot with a new sentence.
4. After prose drafting, check whether any slot is missing.

## Limits

- Do not add a new scene, setting, or character.
- Do not turn values that have not passed the check into prose.
